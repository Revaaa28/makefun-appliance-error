A vacuum cleaner reports a full dustbin — a flat inventory that turns into a confession of theft. One line, 101 characters, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "The Funniest Honest Appliance Error" bounty ($2, winner-takes-all).

The entry: "Error: dustbin full. I have swallowed a sock, three coins and your earring. I am keeping the earring."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## How the line was chosen

Twelve candidate lines (one per appliance + plausible fault) were written to `candidates.md` and ranked manually against the judge brief, in order — honest error, real joke with a twist, plausible fault, the machine's flat put-upon voice, economy — plus pairwise checks against the strongest rival shapes. No automated judge was used for selection.
